What Slimzol is

A liquid dietary supplement, taken once a day

Slimzol is a liquid weight loss support supplement, sold in a 1 Fl Oz (30 mL) bottle with a dropper cap. The label carries the product code SZ-001 and the description Nutritional Support. Each 1 mL dropperful delivers 7 mcg of chromium as chromium picolinate, which is 20% of the Daily Value, together with a 15.47 mg proprietary blend of 22 botanical extracts, amino acids and related compounds.

It is designed to slot into a daily routine without any preparation. There is no capsule to swallow, no powder to measure and nothing to mix: the suggested use on the label is to shake the bottle, take one dropperful, place it in the mouth and swallow. At one serving a day, a 30-serving bottle covers a month.

Most of the botanicals in the blend are concentrated extracts rather than raw herb. The majority are sourced from a concentrate at a 20:1 extract equivalent, and the African mango seed extract at 10:1, which is how a meaningful amount of plant material fits into a single millilitre of liquid.

The Slimzol blend, ingredient by ingredient

22 parts, grouped by what they are

The proprietary blend is listed on the label as a single 15.47 mg total, which is normal for a formula of this kind and means the amount of each individual part is not broken out. What the label does give you is the full list, in descending order. Here is what each one is.

Botanical extracts

Maca root Lepidium meyeniiA root vegetable from the Peruvian Andes, used there as a food and a traditional tonic. First on the list, so the largest botanical share.
African mango seed Irvingia gabonensisThe seed of a West African tree, known locally as dika nut. The one ingredient concentrated at 10:1 rather than 20:1.
Grape seed Vitis viniferaA by-product of winemaking, valued for its proanthocyanidins, a family of plant polyphenols.
Guarana seed Paullinia cupanaAn Amazonian climbing plant whose seed is a natural source of caffeine. This is why Slimzol is not a night-time supplement.
Eleuthero root Eleutherococcus senticosusA shrub from north-east Asia, long used in Russian and Chinese herbal practice and classed as an adaptogen.
Astragalus root Astragalus membranaceusA staple of traditional Chinese herbalism, usually taken as a long-term tonic rather than a short course.
Green tea leafOne of the most widely studied plants in the supplement aisle, supplying catechins and a small amount of natural caffeine.
Gymnema sylvestre leafA woody vine from India, known in Hindi as gurmar. Chewing the leaf famously mutes the taste of sweetness.
Coleus forskohlii rootA member of the mint family native to India, and the source of the compound forskolin.
Cayenne pepper fruit Capsicum annuumThe chilli that supplies capsaicin, the compound responsible for the heat and for the warmth some people notice after a dose.
Grapefruit seed Citrus paradisiAn extract taken from the seed and membrane rather than from the juice.
Panax ginseng rootTrue Asian ginseng, the most researched of the ginsengs. The only botanical listed without an extract-ratio marker.

Amino acids and other compounds

L-carnitine HClAn amino acid derivative the body also makes itself, concentrated in muscle tissue and involved in how cells handle fatty acids.
L-glutamineThe most abundant free amino acid in the bloodstream, and a common ingredient in sports formulas.
L-tyrosineAn amino acid the body uses as a building block for dopamine, adrenaline and thyroid hormones.
L-arginineA semi-essential amino acid, and the body's precursor to nitric oxide.
Beta alanineA non-essential amino acid best known from pre-workout formulas, where it contributes to carnosine.
L-tryptophanAn essential amino acid found in ordinary foods, and the precursor to serotonin.
GABAGamma aminobutyric acid, the main calming neurotransmitter, included here in its supplemental form.
Raspberry ketonesThe aroma compound that gives red raspberries their scent, now produced at supplement scale.
Monoammonium glycyrrhizinateDerived from liquorice root and intensely sweet, so it works as a flavouring as well as a liquorice-derived ingredient.

A note on the liquorice component. Monoammonium glycyrrhizinate comes from liquorice, which some people are advised to limit, particularly anyone managing blood pressure. The amount here is small, inside a 15.47 mg blend, but if liquorice is something you have been told to avoid it is worth raising with your doctor before you start.

Chromium picolinate in Slimzol

7 mcg a serving, 20% of the Daily Value

Chromium is the only ingredient in Slimzol with an established Daily Value, which is why it sits above the blend on the panel with a real percentage beside it. Each dropperful supplies 7 mcg, or 20% DV.

It is an essential trace mineral, meaning the body needs small amounts and cannot make its own. It occurs naturally in foods such as broccoli, whole grains, potatoes, meat and brewer's yeast, usually in very small quantities. The picolinate form used here pairs chromium with picolinic acid, chosen by supplement makers because it is better absorbed than chromium on its own. The NIH Office of Dietary Supplements publishes a plain-language fact sheet on chromium covering what it does, how much people typically get and the research to date.

Because chromium is a nutrient rather than an active drug, a 20% DV contribution is best read as topping up a daily intake rather than doing anything dramatic on its own. If you already take a multivitamin, check whether that contains chromium too, so you know your combined intake.

Storing Slimzol and keeping it fresh

Away from heat, light and humidity

The storage line on the label is specific: keep away from heat, light and humidity to preserve freshness. For a botanical liquid that is not boilerplate. Heat and light degrade plant compounds, and humidity is the reason a bathroom cabinet is the wrong home for it.

  • A cupboard, not a windowsill. Daylight is the most common way a liquid supplement loses potency early.
  • Not the bathroom. Shower humidity is exactly what the label warns about.
  • Cap on firmly. The dropper cap is the seal once the safety seal is off.
  • Out of reach of children, as the label states in capitals.
  • No fridge needed, unless your kitchen runs warm. Cool and dark is the requirement, not cold.
